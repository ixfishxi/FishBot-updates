2.0.8
- Auto → Auto Fishing: shift + right-click at the cursor every 1.0 s (aim at water first).
- Auto → Ice Fishing: scans ice cracks within 6 sq, walks over, picks the hole, fishes until a catch, then ignores that tile. Pauses Cave Walk while working a hole and resumes when done. Use Add crack underfoot in the builder if Mythos tile ids differ from 7200/7236.
- Dark skin works again — Settings → Window shows Restart / Save & Restart when you change skin.
- Fishing rules and the rest of Assist still save in mythos_assist_ui.json next to the exe, so updating FishBot does not wipe your setup.
