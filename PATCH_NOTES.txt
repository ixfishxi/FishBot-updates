2.0.26
- Ice Fishing Wait slot: Drop Regular Fish no longer steals the heavy RPC slot during pick/fish (drop only when idle, release slot if drop fails).
- Ice pick/fish should run the same tick instead of waiting behind its own drop-fish check.

2.0.25
- Cave Walk works again with Auto Ice Fishing on: 2.0.24 blocked all cave movement while Ice was enabled (Stuck N/10).
- Auto Ice defers roaming scans when Cave → Ice Fishing is on; the cave script drives stands, Drop Regular Fish still runs.
- Toggling Auto Ice on no longer cancels an active cave autowalk.

2.0.24
- Auto Ice Fishing holds Cave Walk while enabled so the cave route no longer runs you across the map.
- Drops stale ice jobs when the crack/hole moves outside your Scan sq radius (no long autowalk back).
- Pick and fish always send RPC use-on-tile plus Shift+right-click; stand tile is always north of the crack.
- Ice builder adds Pick and Rod id fields (defaults 3456 / 3483); settings flush when you toggle Ice on.

2.0.23
- Ice Fishing catch detection no longer depends on worm count alone: also checks fish loot increase and treats a closed hole after fishing as Caught.
- Worm count includes the ammo slot (equipped worms); status shows fish loot when tracked.

2.0.22
- Auto cluster (green Auto dot) now runs Ice Fishing, Change Gold, etc. even when the main Master dot is off — Knight2-style setups no longer show a stuck Master off on ice.
- Auto tab header only shows MASTER OFF when both main Master and that cluster are off.

2.0.21
- Auto Ice Fishing runs every tick when enabled (no longer stuck on Waiting while haste/gold/heals use the hotkey slot).
- Ice scan radius defaults to 2 squares; Auto builder has a Scan sq field (1–15).
- Roaming ice fishing uses two-pass tile detection (scan + direct reads) so manually opened holes within range are fished.

2.0.20
- Relaunch Bot picks the newest local FishBot_*.exe (including dist/) instead of staying on python or an older build.
- Quick Action menu items respond on the first click (outside-dismiss no longer eats the press).
- Cave Ice Fishing at stand waypoints works again while the cave is on hold (no more stuck Waiting with no pick/fish).
